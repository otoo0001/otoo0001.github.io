Research statement

My work makes groundwater dependence visible at the global scale, linking physically based groundwater modelling with satellite observations, climate scenarios and ecological evidence.

01

Mapping

Mapping aquatic, wetland and terrestrial groundwater-dependent ecosystems using GLOBGM and PCR-GLOBWB–MODFLOW.

02

Dynamics

Quantifying historical wetland change and separating climate variability, groundwater abstraction and land-use effects.

03

Futures

Projecting groundwater-dependent wetlands under CMIP6 climate and socioeconomic pathways, with implications for biodiversity.
